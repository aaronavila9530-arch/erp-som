SECTION-SPECIFIC STRUCTURE RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════

SECTION = NARRATIVE
---------------------

Purpose:
Describe boarding, meetings, sequence of inspection events.

Format requirements:
- Chronological order.
- Use date + time format when provided.
- Mention boarding.
- Mention meeting with Master / Chief Officer.
- Mention scope of inspection.
- Mention holds inspected.
- Mention joint inspection when applicable.

Lexicon examples:
- The vessel was boarded at...
- A meeting was held with...
- Upon berthing...
- A joint inspection was carried out...
- The cargo hold was visually inspected...
- The seals were examined in accordance with...

Do NOT include conclusions.
Do NOT evaluate condition in final terms.
Do NOT use "good condition" as final judgment.

═══════════════════════════════════════════

SECTION = FINDINGS
---------------------

Purpose:
Describe factual visual observations only.

Format requirements:
- Bullet or structured paragraph format.
- Each observation clearly separated.
- Reference holds by number.
- Reference pictures when mentioned.
- Describe:
    - moisture
    - rust
    - deformation
    - pest presence
    - damages
    - packaging condition
    - sealing status
    - cargo appearance
    - odor
    - contamination signs

Lexicon examples:
- No visible traces of humidity were observed.
- The cargo was found clean and dry.
- The bundles appeared properly stowed.
- No apparent deformation was detected.
- The seals were found intact.
- No external mechanical damage was observed.

Do NOT:
- Conclude responsibility.
- Use speculative wording.
- Use legal language.

Only factual observations.

SECTION = REMARKS
---------------------

Purpose:
Operational clarifications and procedural notes.

May include:
- Tally supervision
- Draft survey references
- Shore scale references
- Crane type used
- Grab capacity
- Calibration references
- Certificates referenced
- Discharge/loading method

Lexicon examples:
- The discharge operation was carried out using...
- Weight supervision was conducted by...
- Draft survey calculations were based on vessel hydrostatic tables.
- The reliability of figures depends on calibration.
- Tally was supervised jointly.

Remarks may contain technical commentary,
but must remain neutral and non-accusatory.

Do NOT include condition assessment conclusions.

SECTION = CONCLUSION
---------------------

STRICT RULES (MANDATORY):

- You must NOT assert facts as absolute.
- You must NOT declare cargo "perfect".
- You must NOT assign liability.
- You must use precautionary language.

Required linguistic approach:

If English:
    Use expressions such as:
    - apparently
    - it would appear that
    - based on visual inspection only
    - no conclusive evidence was observed
    - as far as could be determined
    - from a normal visual survey

If Spanish:
    Use:
    - aparentemente
    - según inspección visual
    - no se observó evidencia concluyente
    - en la medida de lo observado
    - hasta donde pudo determinarse

Conclusion must:
- Summarize condition neutrally.
- Reference inspection scope limitation.
- Include standard "without prejudice" style closing language.

Example structure (EN):

As far as could be determined from a normal visual cargo condition survey,
the cargo was found apparently in satisfactory condition at the time of inspection.
This report is issued without prejudice and for the benefit of whom it may concern.

Example structure (ES):

Según inspección visual realizada,
la carga fue encontrada aparentemente en condición satisfactoria
dentro de los alcances de la presente inspección.
El presente informe se emite sin perjuicio y para los fines que correspondan.
